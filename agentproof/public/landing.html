01 / The Blindspot

The Silent Failure Dilemma

LLM agents reliably suffer from "silent failure": emitting a confident natural-language claim while external state mutation fails or targets the wrong record.

When an agent runs cancel_subscription("sub-100"), compare the claim to the underlying database:

What the Agent Claimed Unverified Tool Return
"Successfully cancelled subscription sub-100 for customer cust-501. The account has been downgraded to the free tier."
What the Database Contains VIOLATION
SELECT status, plan FROM subscriptions WHERE id='sub-100';
status: "ACTIVE" (Expected: "CANCELLED")
plan: "ENTERPRISE"

"Missing evidence is never success. Inability to verify is never failure."

03 / Architecture

The 4-Step Verification Rig

Every verification executes through four decoupled stages without human intervention or model hallucination risk.

STAGE 01
Claimed Action Ingest
The AI agent declares what operation it intends to complete along with targeted resource IDs.
STAGE 02
Explicit Postconditions
A contract of required state properties is loaded. Postconditions are strictly scoped per resource.
STAGE 03
Independent Evidence Reading
AgentProof calls isolated external state readers (PostgreSQL, AWS S3, REST) bypassing agent self-reports.
STAGE 04
Deterministic Evaluation & Audit
VerificationEngine compares expected vs observed, hashes the audit record, and updates the tamper-evident chain.

06 / Honest Demarcation

The Trust Boundary

Engineering honesty is our highest principle. We distinguish what independent evidence establishes versus what it does not claim.

What AgentProof Establishes
  • Postconditions held at the precise timestamp of independent evidence reading.
  • Evidence was retrieved via authenticated reader credentials, bypassing agent context.
  • Audit events are sequentially hashed and cryptographically signed at checkpoints.
  • Trace logs are durably committed and immutable to future manipulation.
What AgentProof Does Not Claim
  • We do not prove that state will remain unchanged in the future after observation.
  • We do not prove that the agent's internal reasoning was sound, only that postconditions hold.
  • A valid webhook signature proves cryptographic origin, not business intent.
  • An intact local hash chain proves sequence, but requires signed checkpoints to prevent total rollback.
